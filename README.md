🧠 About Me
I'm a final-year ECE student at IIIT Nagpur, passionate about building intelligent embedded systems and IoT solutions that solve real-world problems — from smart homes to industrial safety monitors.
I don't just write code; I wire up circuits, solder boards, and make hardware think.
🔬 I specialize in Microcontrollers, Sensor Integration & Real-Time Systems
⚡ I love working at the intersection of hardware and software
🛠️ I've built projects spanning smart home automation, precision agriculture, and predictive maintenance
🌱 Currently exploring BLE, MQTT & advanced PCB design
📍 Based in Andhra Pradesh, India — building systems that actually work in the real world
